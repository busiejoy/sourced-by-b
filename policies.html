HOW IT WORKS

Placing an Order

01. Tell Us What You Want

Browse our catalogue or send us a picture, link or description of the product you want.

02. Receive a Quotation

We'll check availability and provide a quotation for you to review.

03. Confirm Your Order

Once you agree to the quotation, pay a 50% deposit to confirm your order.

04. Complete Your Payment

Pay the remaining 50% according to the payment arrangement agreed upon with us.

PLEASE TAKE NOTE

Our Policies

Payment Policy

A 50% deposit is required after you agree to the quotation. The remaining balance is payable according to the arrangement agreed upon before your order is confirmed.

Product Availability

Products displayed in our catalogue are examples of styles we can help you source. Availability must be confirmed before an order is accepted.

Delivery Policy

Delivery arrangements, estimated timelines and applicable fees depend on your location. These details will be communicated before you confirm your order.

Quotations

No prices are displayed publicly on our website. Contact us for a quotation based on your requested product and delivery arrangements.

GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you sell only the products in your catalogue?

No! You can contact us about other fashion products you would like us to help you source.

How do I find out the price?

Send us an enquiry on WhatsApp. We'll check the product details and provide a quotation.

Can I order an item that isn't displayed?

Yes. Send us a product picture, link or description so we can check availability.

How much is delivery?

Delivery fees depend on your location and the available delivery arrangement. We'll communicate the applicable fee beforehand.
