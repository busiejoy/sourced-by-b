SIMPLE FROM START TO FINISH

How it works.

Finding what you want shouldn't feel complicated. Here's how we make it easier.

01 ⌕

Tell us what you want

Choose an item from our catalogue or send us a description, picture or link of what you need.

02 ↗

Receive your quotation

We'll check availability and send you a private quotation for you to review before proceeding.

03 ♡

Confirm your order

Once you agree to the quotation, pay a 50% deposit to confirm your order.

04 ✳

Complete your payment

Pay the remaining 50% according to the payment arrangement agreed with us.

Please note: Availability, final pricing, delivery arrangements and payment details are confirmed with you before your order proceeds. Your deposit confirms your order after you have accepted the quotation.
